EduApp Fast Clicker

See ETH EduApp clicker questions within seconds and get your answer through, even on bad lecture-hall Wi-Fi. Nothing to install: it's a bookmark you tap while EduApp is open.

Set it up once (1 minute)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it's hidden: Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: ⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this yellow button onto the bookmarks bar:

    ⚡ Clicker
    Clicking it here does nothing; it must be dragged. Can't drag? Use copy the code and create a bookmark named “Clicker” with the code as its address.
  3. Done. In the lecture: open EduApp, log in, then click ⚡ Clicker in your bookmarks bar.

In the lecture

  • Open EduApp, tap Clicker. EduApp switches to a clean full-screen view and stops its own background downloads.
  • Tap the screen once so the alert sound can play. Keep the tab open: the screen stays on during your lectures.
  • When the lecturer opens a question it pops up with a sound (usually within 2–5 seconds). Tap your answer, then Send.
  • “Sent ✓” means the server accepted it, and “✓ Confirmed” means it was double-checked. On bad Wi-Fi it keeps retrying for 2 minutes on its own, and your answer survives connection drops and an expired login.
  • Optional: 1-tap send sends single-choice answers as soon as you tap them.

Why it's faster and more reliable

  • The normal EduApp never checks for new questions by itself, and every refresh downloads all clicker questions of the semester (over 1 MB). Fast Clicker checks a ~2 KB activity feed every few seconds around your lectures and only downloads the question that just opened.
  • EduApp gives up after one failed send and reloads the whole page when your login expires, losing your answer. Fast Clicker retries on its own, renews your login in the background when ETH's single sign-on allows it, and confirms the answer with the server.

Safe and private

  • Runs only in your own browser tab on eduapp.ethz.ch, with your normal EduApp login. It never sees your password and sends nothing anywhere else.
  • It never answers for you. It only sends what you tap.
  • Considerate by design: during your lectures it makes one small request every ~4 s (every 15 s in a background tab, about once a minute otherwise), one at a time. Each request still costs the EduApp server about a second of work, so the load adds up if a whole lecture hall uses it. It slows down automatically as soon as EduApp answers slower than usual. Please don't modify it to check more often.
  • Unofficial: made by a student, not by ETH. If EduApp changes, it may stop working until updated.
Questions

Why not a normal app or website? Browsers only let code use your EduApp login when it runs on eduapp.ethz.ch itself. A separate site would need your ETH password, and you should never give that to anyone. The bookmark runs inside EduApp, so it doesn't need it.

Do I have to tap the bookmark every lecture? Once per EduApp tab. If you reload the page, tap it again. On a laptop you can instead use the browser add-on below, which starts automatically.

Several EduApp tabs? Fine. Only one of them checks for questions and the others get its updates.

“Login expired”? Tap Log in, log in, and come back. Your chosen answer is kept and sent right after.

Browser add-on for laptops (starts automatically)

Same code, shown as a small panel on every EduApp page, with a “Focus mode” button. Until it's published in the Chrome/Firefox add-on stores, installing it needs developer mode:

  1. Download the add-on (.zip) and unzip it.
  2. Chrome/Edge/Brave: open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, choose the unzipped folder.
